Get Involved

Join our network, volunteer your time or support our work. There is a place for everyone at Hands 4 Unity.

Why join us

What our network offers

  • Funding access

    Access to funding to support your own community projects.

  • Networking

    Information sharing, support and expert advice through our network.

  • Social events

    Opportunities to attend social events with other community groups in Ireland and abroad.

  • Job seekers support

    Career advice, support and details of job opportunities in your community.

  • Newsletter

    A monthly newsletter with upcoming projects and organisation updates.
