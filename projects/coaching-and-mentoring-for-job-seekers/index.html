Coaching and Mentoring for Job Seekers

ProgrammeOngoingDrogheda

Free, practical support for migrant and Irish women who want to enter the workforce, return to work or change career.

Looking for a job in Ireland and not sure where to start?

You don't have to do it alone. At Hands4Unity, we support migrant and Irish women who want to enter the workforce, return to employment or change careers, with real, practical guidance every step of the way.

Our employment-focused support programme offers:

  • One-to-one coaching to define your goals and build your confidence.
  • CV clinics to help you create or improve your CV.
  • Interview preparation with real Irish context and feedback.
  • Mentorship from professionals who know the Irish job market.
  • Navigation support to help you understand how recruitment works here.

Whether you've never worked in Ireland, or you're just feeling stuck or overlooked, we'll help you unlock your potential and take the next step toward your career goals.

Who is it for?

This programme is open to anyone currently looking for work, whether you're actively applying, considering a return to employment, or simply want to better understand the Irish job market. We especially welcome:

  • Migrant and refugee job seekers, including those still building their English or adjusting to a new work culture.
  • Irish job seekers facing barriers such as long-term unemployment, lack of qualifications or low confidence.
  • Women returning to work after maternity leave, caregiving or a career break.
  • Young adults and recent graduates looking for guidance in entering the workforce.
  • People changing careers or reorienting after redundancy or life changes.

Whether you have no experience or many years behind you, you are welcome here. Our approach is inclusive, human-centred and practical, with a strong focus on helping you take clear, confident steps forward.

Book a free appointment

No matter where you're starting from, your journey matters, and we're here to support it. Whether you need help polishing your CV, preparing for interviews or figuring out where to begin, our team is ready to guide you.

Book a free one-to-one appointment and take the first step toward your future with confidence. Together, we'll work on what you need, at your pace, in a safe and supportive environment.
