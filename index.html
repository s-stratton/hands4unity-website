Empowering women, uniting communities.

Hands 4 Unity is a support network for women and families of all backgrounds, in Ireland and abroad. We help women find work, learn new skills and build a sense of belonging.

Coaching & mentoring

Looking for a job in Ireland and not sure where to start?

You don't have to do it alone.

We support migrant and Irish women who want to enter the workforce, return to employment or change careers, with real, practical guidance every step of the way.

  • One-to-one coaching to build your confidence
  • CV clinics and interview preparation
  • Mentors who know the Irish job market

What we do

Networking, education, job access and social support

Fostering economic empowerment and inclusive integration for migrant and host community women through networking, education, job access and social support.

  • Job seekers help

    Free CV clinics, mentoring and coaching sessions for job seekers.

  • Online healthcare

    Access to online healthcare for women in less privileged communities.

  • Support to communities

    Encouraging members to tackle health and educational issues in their communities.

  • Integration

    Rehabilitation and integration services for women in need.

Meet our founder

Cllr Ejiro O'Hare Stratton

Founder & CEO

Ejiro Stratton, the founder of Hands4Unity CLG, is a dynamic and compassionate leader in Drogheda, recognised with the Outstanding Individual Award for Louth in November 2023.

Ejiro leads a board and committee of volunteers from across Drogheda's communities, including Ireland, Nigeria, India and the Philippines.
